Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Core Agency Ltd. (“Core Agency”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers this website only. Data you store in the Core Agency POS software is covered by your service agreement with us.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the demo request form:

  • your business name, your name and your phone number;
  • your email address, city or area, preferred time to call and any notes, if you provide them;
  • your business type and number of outlets.

Like almost every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when the site is visited, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These server logs are used only to keep the site secure and working.

What we don’t do

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics trackers or advertising pixels.
  • Our fonts and scripts are served from our own server, so visiting the site does not send your data to third-party font or script providers.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it for anyone else’s marketing.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your demo request and arrange the demo.
  • To prepare the demo for your business, for example by loading the products and prices you send us.
  • To answer questions you ask us, and to follow up about Core Agency products you have shown interest in. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Services that handle your request

When you submit the form, your request is delivered to our email inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that processes it only to send it to us. If you choose to continue on WhatsApp, your message is handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. We use email and WhatsApp only to communicate with you about your request.

How long we keep it

We keep demo requests for as long as we need them to respond to you and to manage our relationship with you. If you don’t become a customer, we delete your request when it is no longer needed for that purpose, or sooner if you ask us to.

How we protect it

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to the requests we receive is limited to the Core Agency staff who need it to contact you.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us at hello@coreagency.com.bd and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
